The Atom Engine

At the core lies the Atom—a single, observable source of truth. Combined with a sophisticated DAG-based dependency tracker, Atom Effect ensures your application only updates exactly what is needed, precisely when it is needed. No Virtual DOM overhead, just pure performance.

Seamless Binding

Bringing modern reactive paradigms to the world's most trusted DOM library. With atom-effect-jquery, you can bind your reactive state directly to jQuery selections with a single line of code. Declarative UI development meets the power of jQuery.
